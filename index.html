How CredentialFlow works

One connected workflow from upload through management action.

1
Upload Worker submits a credential.
2

3
Track Status and renewal dates stay visible.
4

5
Decide Management can review and record an outcome.

The problem → The opportunity

Before CredentialFlow

  • Documents spread across different systems.
  • Managers manually chase workers.
  • Renewal dates can be difficult to track.
  • Overdue explanations may arrive through different channels.
  • Management spends time following up instead of focusing on people.

With CredentialFlow

  • One organised credential workflow.
  • Clear visual status for every credential.
  • Upload and renewal information in one place.
  • Structured overdue and extension requests.
  • Management receives clearer actions and decisions.
