CredentialFlow

Workforce credential renewal and verification, simplified.

CredentialFlow is an open-source concept and prototype designed to reduce the administrative burden of managing workforce credentials and renewals.

It was inspired by real-world experience from both sides of the process — as a support worker and as an administration worker.

The Problem

Managing employee credentials can become unnecessarily time-consuming for both workers and administration teams.

Workers may be:

* Working during the day and unable to respond immediately
* Forgetting to send documents after being reminded
* Having difficulty attaching documents to emails
* Sending documents to the wrong staff member or email address
* Unsure whether their document has actually been received or processed

At the same time, administration teams may spend significant time:

* Calling and messaging workers for outstanding documents
* Searching through emails for previously submitted credentials
* Following up on documents that have already been sent
* Manually updating employee records
* Determining whether an employee has responded to a renewal notification
* Managing requests for additional time when a credential is delayed

CredentialFlow aims to bring this process into one central workflow.

The Solution

CredentialFlow is designed to allow workers to manage required credentials through the same digital environment they already use for their work.

Workers can:

* 🔔 Receive renewal notifications
* ✅ Acknowledge renewal alerts
* 📤 Upload updated credentials
* 👀 View the status of submitted documents
* 📝 Explain why a credential is overdue or unavailable
* 📅 Request an extension to a specific date
* 🔎 See whether their document is pending, approved or requires further action

Administrators can:

* 📊 View workforce credential status in real time
* 📥 Review submitted documents
* ✅ Approve or reject credentials
* 📝 Review extension requests
* 🔔 Monitor upcoming and overdue credentials
* 📜 Maintain an audit history of submissions and decisions
* 🔄 Keep credential records updated from a central workflow

Example Credentials

CredentialFlow could support documents such as:

* CPR certificates

* Vehicle insurance

Design Principles

CredentialFlow is being designed around:

Simplicity
Make credential management easy for workers and administrators.

Transparency
Workers should be able to see whether a document has been received, reviewed or approved.

Accountability
Important actions should create a clear audit trail.

Efficiency
Reduce unnecessary phone calls, emails and manual administrative work.

Privacy
Credential information is sensitive and must be handled securely.

Human verification
Automated checks may assist with document processing, but final approval should remain with an appropriately authorised person.

Project Status

🚧 Early prototype

This project is currently in the design and development stage.

The initial prototype will use fictional employee information and sample documents only.

Vision

CredentialFlow aims to demonstrate how workforce credential management could become simpler, more transparent and less stressful for both employees and administration teams.

The long-term vision is to create a flexible system that could work alongside existing workforce-management platforms rather than requiring organisations to replace the systems they already use.
