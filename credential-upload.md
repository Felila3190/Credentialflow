Credential Upload

Purpose

CredentialFlow makes it easier for workers to upload, manage and keep track of their required workplace credentials and compliance documents.

The Problem

Workers can be required to provide multiple documents such as:

* Qualifications and certificates
* First aid certificates
* Working with Children checks
* NDIS Worker Screening checks
* Driver licences
* Training certificates
* Other workplace-required credentials

Keeping these documents organised and knowing when they need to be renewed can be difficult.

CredentialFlow Solution

CredentialFlow is designed to provide a simple workflow where a worker can:

1. Upload a credential or certificate.
2. Store the document securely.
3. Record the issue and expiry dates.
4. Receive reminders before a credential expires.
5. Easily provide verification information to an employer or organisation.
6. Keep their compliance information organised in one place.

Goal

The goal is to reduce administrative chasing, make credential uploading easier for workers, and help organisations keep workforce compliance information organised.
